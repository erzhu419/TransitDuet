# V69: event-coupled continuation variance pilot

Freeze the main controller, V59 actors, upper/B200 deployment, dual and value
models. Test only how future categorical lower-policy draws are paired across
counterfactual actions; do not fit or promote a controller.

Predeclare 32 V67 prefixes: all four actors and eight demand days, with target
window `day_index % 3` (12 early, 12 middle, 8 late). Reuse their eight V68
future seeds and stream-coupled controls. Run all seven actions under the new
coupling: 256 groups / 1,792 new formal rollouts, 1,792 existing controls.

After the original current action is drawn and its full V67 prefix matched,
fork exogenous/runtime streams exactly as V68. Replace only subsequent lower
categorical sampling with inverse-CDF sampling from its unchanged masked
probabilities. Independent uniforms are generated by NumPy SeedSequence from
`[future_seed+10000000, trip_id, stop_id, direction, local_occurrence]`.
Clock time, physical bus ID and global decision order are not in this key.
Repeated visits use their local occurrence, not a reused draw. Record common
logical events, their identical uniforms and shifted order/times/actions.

Smoke on two original training seeds: focused tests, one unchanged V68 stream
endpoint identity, two complete event-coupled grids and forced-native full
holding/upper/day identities. Seventeen replays, fourteen reused formally.
All tests/replays/aggregation run through scheduler on node001-node006. Pull
only small completion/status JSON and necessary log tails.

Primary endpoint: paired action-difference noise variance versus zero holding,
excluding the zero arm from averaging, event/stream ratio and paired difference.
Bootstrap the eight original demand-day clusters, averaging four actors within
each day. Secondary: corrected signal squared, half-future sign agreement,
the unchanged V67 action on new continuations, and 4-versus-4 future selection
with passenger/CV-squared/readiness/unlaunched/incomplete constraints intact.
Passenger units are full-day person-seconds averaged across prefixes.

Decision: retain coupling for label collection only if the passenger-variance
ratio CI is below one and CV-squared variance does not significantly worsen;
otherwise close this variance hypothesis without fitting a scorer. Native
endpoint differences diagnose gross law changes, not statistical equivalence.
Any subsequent control claim needs independent prefixes and confirmation.

Limitations: consumed development prefixes, eight futures and two original
training seeds; conditional on the fixed realized day profile and already
sampled pending arrivals. Event-key pairing tests avoidable Monte Carlo noise,
not an established bug or a demonstrated improvement in main-policy service.
