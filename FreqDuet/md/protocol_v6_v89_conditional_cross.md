# V89: causal conditional actor replacement

V88 concentrates common-state excess holding in valid bilateral contexts where
the observable local regularity surrogate does not improve. This identifies
an allocation hypothesis, not useless actions: a short/local surrogate may
miss full-day benefits. Test dynamic regimes rather than tune constant biases
or suppress individual actions using these observational labels.

Freeze all actor/critic/dual weights and B200 upper/deployment background. Use
the unmodified native seven-bin early and late lower laws on each current state.
Cross early-law replacement in the two V88 positive-extra-holding groups:
no predicted gain only; predicted gain only; both; neither (native late).
Retain native early as a fifth reference. Missing bilateral evidence and
no-extra-holding states keep late in all conditional arms. This is a causal
diagnostic intervention in experimental action laws, not a main-policy gate.

Lock the primary contrast to no-gain-only versus late. Gain-only and the
factorial interaction test specificity; other arms cannot replace a failed
primary through winner selection. Evaluate passenger person-time, journey,
full-day arrival CV-squared, readiness, unlaunched/incomplete trips and holding.
Record whether the unchanged joint passenger-improvement/nonharm diagnostic
criterion passes, but do not admit any controller from this pilot.

Use new demand days 1891001-1891016, both original actors, three logical-event
matched samplers: 96 cells, five arms each, 480 full-day rollouts. Day-paired
intervals are conditional on two actors; this is not training-seed confirmation
or natural mediation. Classification is recomputed on actual current states;
full closed-loop continuation and the reactive frozen upper remain active.

Record baseline joint context counts for nonzero onboard load, current return
pressure and prior commanded holding. These natural-zero bins resolve the
joint-frequency information absent from V88 weighted moments; they do not
enter the primary intervention. Smoke must reproduce V88 native metrics and
full event/actual-holding paths for both actors/corners, and execute a real
primary intervention without increasing its instantaneous expected holding or
reducing its local surrogate. That local property is not a full-day CV promise.
All tests, smoke, rollouts and statistics use scheduler on node001-node006.
Keep detailed data/checkpoints server-side and retrieve small summaries only.
