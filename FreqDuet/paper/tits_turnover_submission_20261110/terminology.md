coordination executable; independent comparisons establish operational tradeoffs,
but not a pressure-allocation or learned-supervisor advantage at preserved regularity.

## Claim--Evidence Map

- Budget configuration changes journey/circulation and regularity: Study A,
  supported by its original primary gate.
- Learned-supervisor journey superiority: Study A, not established; tighter
  retention favors manual joint. Study B converges to identical joint behavior.
- Harmonic journey advantage: Study A, primary gate failed; secondary CV2 favors K4.
- Pressure credit improves delay without CV2 harm against simple limits: Study C,
  original gate failed against both families.
- Common-reference admission resolves that conflict: Study D, development screen failed.
- Interface invariants: chronological accounting and analytical identities;
  not proof of full-day control optimality.
- Factorial and four conditions: budget improves circulation, with regularity
  cost; the current timetable law has no consistent extra service advantage.
- Heavy-demand graph support: zero; native fallback is still projected by
  issued credit. Do not attribute those gains to supported model optimization.
