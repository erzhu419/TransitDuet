# Current Paper Entry

The current evidence-aligned draft is
[the turnover-coordination manuscript](tits_turnover_submission_20261110/README.md).
It incorporates V400--V404 and the separate V412--V417 follow-up in the
Discussion and supporting information, preserving the original positive and
failed gates.
The new factorial, operating-condition, passenger-distribution and vehicle-chain
evidence is assembled with the main/SI PDFs, editable figures and source package.
It is a bounded operating-tradeoff draft, not a validated learned/frequency
superiority paper. The experiment main policy is unchanged.

The separate [V408 confirmation](../experiments/v408_heavy_confirmation/RESULTS.md)
establishes a scoped fast-holding result under 1.2x demand: frozen V406
improves restricted journey, actual CV2, readiness and public delay against
the prespecified whole-day cap mixture. The pure-cap contrasts retain
tradeoffs. This follow-up is not part of the archived V400--V404 PDFs and
does not establish a learned-supervisor, timetable or frequency advantage.

[V409](../experiments/v409_holding_dose/RESULTS.md) shows a joint benefit on
independent demand days with a shared 10 s ceiling and comparable
mean commanded holding against a frozen daily mixture. The unrestricted
candidate did not pass holding equivalence. Both findings are supplemental;
neither changes the archived paper mainline or promotes a policy.

[V410](../experiments/v410_within_day_control/RESULTS.md) repeats the
high-demand fast-holding comparison with a within-day randomized rule
instead of a whole-day mixture. The capped controller improves journey,
actual CV2 and circulation at matched mean commanded holding and 0/5/10
s shares. The result concerns the tested state-dependent fast controller
and remains separate from the archived V400--V404 manuscript.

[V411](../experiments/v411_demand_transfer/RESULTS.md) transfers the frozen
V410 comparison to 1.35x demand with all matching and outcome gates passed.
At nominal demand all outcome gates pass but holding equivalence does not.
These are single-corridor results; they do not change the manuscript or policy.

[V412](../experiments/v412_running_speed_transfer/RESULTS.md) reproduces the
joint matched-dose result at normal speed on new days. Under slower running,
matching, journey and actual CV2 pass, but circulation nonharm does not.
This is evidence for a bounded service benefit, not a joint robustness claim.

[V414](../experiments/v413_public_slot_observer/RUNNING_SHADOW.md) improves
five-minute-ahead actual departure-time predictions on eight new slow days.
[V415](../experiments/v415_running_closed_loop/PROTOCOL.md) fails the separate
closed-loop screen. [V416](../experiments/v416_dispatch_spacing/PROTOCOL.md)
improves journey and arrival regularity relative to unprotected hourly
prediction, but misses the pooled arrival-CV2 criterion.
[V417](../experiments/v417_pooled_spacing/PROTOCOL.md) completes the same-day
factorial, retaining the circulation--regularity trade-off. None promotes a
controller or changes the V400--V404 primary claims.

The earlier dated T-ITS drafts and Protocol-V6/TR-C packages remain historical
snapshots. Their abstract, active-controller names and submission status must
not be substituted for the current evidence.
