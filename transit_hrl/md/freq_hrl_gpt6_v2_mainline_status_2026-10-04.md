# Freq-HRL GPT6 v2 Mainline Status

Date: 2026-10-04

## Decision

The original forecast-anchored frequency-separated upper-learning route is
closed. Stages 113--116 all failed to establish a reproducible upper-plan gain;
adding residual capacity, decision-aligned credit, or complete Bernstein plan
coordinates did not change that conclusion.

The strongest surviving result is narrower: Stage-8C qualified a causal
plan-validity predictor, and independent Stage-9 development/confirmation
qualified a same-budget closed-loop renewal trigger on hidden-regime PointMaze.
This is a plan-validity result, not evidence for frequency-band ownership,
full actor-critic HRL, or domain generality.

## Evidence Boundary

- Stage-8B history predictor: registered utility comparison inconclusive.
- Stage-8C compact causal predictor: all seven registered local-value checks
  supported; no episode-level claim.
- Stage-9 confirmation: fixed-minus-candidate ISE and candidate-minus-fixed
  return both supported on fresh PointMaze roots, with exact one-call-per-bin
  budgets and variable option durations.
- Stage-10 learned termination: failed against fixed planning; retain as a
  negative strong baseline result.
- Stage-29/30: action-conditioned physical response and separate external
  motion forecasting qualify, but the shared history predictor gate failed.
- Stage-33/34/35: local response qualification did not transfer to a reliable
  full-episode or jointly trained renewal policy.

## Next Admissible Work

Do not launch another upper residual or joint-renewal sweep. The next genuine
algorithm step must be a fresh, separately registered transfer study: freeze
the Stage-9 causal trigger, implement the same belief/decision/action clock
separation in a second domain, and compare against fixed planning and a strong
learned-termination baseline under matched planning and inference cost. Until
that exists, the defensible claim is a PointMaze plan-validity protocol, not a
general Freq-HRL algorithm.
