## Limitations
The legacy rollout resets global Torch RNG at each lower call, so matching policy_seed alone shares only the first upper innovation; this experiment replays the full recorded sequence instead. Shared innovations are not fixed upper actions and reduce independent upper draws per scenario; any gain need not be a pure covariance effect. This is conditional lower learning with fixed uppers on the same teachers and training scenarios, not joint HRL, independent training replication or equal-total-compute comparison.
